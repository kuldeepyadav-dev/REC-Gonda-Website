    Maa Pateshwari Devi, Rajkiya Engineering College, Gonda

    Maa Pateshwari Devi Rajkiya Engineering College, Gonda, was established by the Government of Uttar Pradesh in 2023 as an associated institute of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), Lucknow. The institute offers four-year full-time B.Tech programmes in Computer Science and Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering, each with an annual intake of 60 students. The institute also promotes innovation and startups, where students work on local problems and develop solutions with wider applications. The institute provides quality education and skills to prepare students to become problem-solvers and globally competitive professionals.
